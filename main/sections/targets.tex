\chapter{Evaluation Targets}\label{chap:targets}

We evaluate our fuzzer against six targets, three belonging to the input action model
and three belonging to the corpus action model.
\Cref{tab:targets} summarizes them.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption[Evaluation targets]{%
    The six evaluation targets.
    Three belong to the input-action model and serve as checks whether a single
    coverage choice, a long action sequence and a protocol state machine can each be
    successfully learned.
    The remaining three belong to the corpus-action model and are used to evaluate
    the effectiveness of iteratively mutating a single seed.
  }
  \label{tab:targets}
  \footnotesize
  \setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}Xrr>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}Xl@{}}
    \toprule
    Target & Program under test & $|\mathcal{A}|$ & Steps & Reward & Role \\
    \midrule
    \multicolumn{6}{@{}l}{\emph{Input actions (\cref{sec:targets-input-actions})}} \\
    high-and-low
      & branch on a single byte
      & 256 & 128
      & raw sum of block hit counts
      & sanity \\
    sequence
      & 16-character passcode
      & 26 & 16
      & living, terminal coverage
      & sanity \\
    open62541
      & \code{tutorial\_server\_firststeps}
      & 15 & 12
      & living, terminal bonus
      & statefulness \\
    \addlinespace
    \multicolumn{6}{@{}l}{\emph{Corpus actions (\cref{sec:targets-corpus-actions})}} \\
    cJSON
      & \code{cJSON\_ParseWithLength}
      & 256 & 128
      & marginal coverage, total coverage
      & ablation \\
    libxml2
      & \code{xmlReadMemory}
      & 256 & 128
      & marginal coverage, total coverage
      & transfer \\
    picohttpparser
      & \code{phr\_parse\_request}
      & 256 & 128
      & marginal coverage, total coverage
      & transfer \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
