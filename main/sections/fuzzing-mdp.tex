\chapter{Approach}

Building a fuzzer on top of EfficientZero requires modeling the fuzzing process
as a \gls{pomdp} $M_F=(\mathcal{S}_F,\mathcal{A}_F,p_F,\mathcal{O}_F,o_F)$ that respects
the capabilities of the underlying execution environment.
All approaches are situated inside an episodic, discounted setting.
